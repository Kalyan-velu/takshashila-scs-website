## 5. 3-Year Integrated Foundation & Civil Services Programme

A progressive programme that moves from foundation, to integration, to target-specific specialisation, and is suitable for students entering after Class X/XII or during graduation.

| Stage | Duration | Primary Objective | Student Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation I | Year 1 | Build basic knowledge and learning habits | Strong academic foundation |
| Foundation II | Year 2 | Develop civil services-level understanding | Integrated GS foundation |
| Specialisation | Year 3 | Target-specific preparation | UPSC / APSC / ADRE / other government exam readiness |

**Learning progression:** Learn > Understand > Connect > Analyse > Write > Test > Compete

| Level | Student Develops |
|---|---|
| Level 1 - Awareness | What is it? |
| Level 2 - Understanding | Why does it happen? |
| Level 3 - Analysis | What are its implications? |
| Level 4 - Application | How do I answer a question on it? |
