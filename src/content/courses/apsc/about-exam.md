## About the APSC (CCE) Examination

The Assam Public Service Commission Combined Competitive Examination (APSC CCE) is the highest-level state exam conducted to recruit Class-I and Class-II officers for the Government of Assam.

The exam process aligns closely with the UPSC format and occurs in three phases:

### Phase I: Preliminary Examination

An objective test serving as a qualifier.

- **Paper I (General Studies I):** 200 Marks (30% to 35% questions are exclusively based on Assam).
- **Paper II (General Studies II):** 200 Marks (Qualifying CSAT paper, requiring 33% to pass).

### Phase II: Mains Examination

A descriptive test determining your core ranking.

- **Paper I:** Essay Writing (250 Marks)
- **Paper II:** General Studies I (Indian Heritage, History and Geography of the World and Society)
- **Paper III:** General Studies II (Governance, Constitution, Polity, Social Justice and International relations)
- **Paper IV:** General Studies III (Technology, Economic Development, Bio-diversity, Environment, Security and Disaster Management)
- **Paper V:** General Studies IV (Ethics, Integrity and Aptitude)
- **Paper VI:** General Studies V (Assam-specific Paper)
  _(Note: APSC has removed Optional Papers for Mains)_

### Phase III: Interview (Personality Test)

- **Marks:** 275 Marks
- Evaluates mental caliber, logical exposition, balance of judgment, and social traits of the candidate.
