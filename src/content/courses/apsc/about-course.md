## APSC Comprehensive Package

Our **APSC (Combined Competitive Examination) Strategy Package** is intricately designed for aspirants seeking to become ACS, APS, and allied officers in Assam. We deeply integrate the **Assam-specific** syllabus into this exhaustive course.

### Highlights

- **Course Duration:** 9 – 10 months
- **Learning Modes:** Select between focused **Offline** batches or flexible **Online** classes.
- **Fees & Payment:** Enroll seamlessly using our flexible **Installment** plans.

### Course Perks

- Complete coverage of Assam History, Geography, and Culture (as per the new APSC pattern).
- High-yield printed & digital Study Materials.
- Scheduled Prelims and Mains Mock Tests simulating the exact APSC environment.
- Recorded backup of all lectures.
- Specialized **Assam Tribune** current affairs analysis and Assam Yearbook coverage.
- Expert MOCK interview panel consisting of distinguished civil servants.
