## Astra - SSC-CGL Batch

Our comprehensive **Astra - SSC-CGL Batch** is specifically designed for candidates targeting the Staff Selection Commission - Combined Graduate Level examination.

---

### Course Highlights & Format

- **Course Duration:** 5-6 Months
- **Total Teaching Hours:** Approximately 180 Hours
- **Batch Intake:** 40 students
- **Teaching Days:** Monday to Friday, 2 hours per day (10 hours per week)
- **Weekend Schedule:** Saturday features weekly tests (2 hours), revision, performance evaluation and mentorship. Sunday is a holiday.
- **Course Fee:** ₹1,999 per student

---

### Academic Coverage (180 Hours)

- **Quantitative Aptitude:** 50 Hours
- **General Intelligence & Reasoning:** 45 Hours
- **English Comprehension:** 40 Hours
- **General Awareness:** 30 Hours
- **Computer Knowledge:** 15 Hours

---

### Subject-wise Topic List

- **Quantitative Aptitude / Mathematics:** Number system; percentage; ratio and proportion; average; profit and loss; simple and compound interest; time and work; time, speed and distance; mensuration; basic algebra; data interpretation.
- **Logical Reasoning & Mental Ability:** Series; analogy; classification; coding-decoding; blood relations; direction sense; syllogism; seating arrangement; logical reasoning.
- **English:** Grammar; vocabulary; reading comprehension; paragraph writing; précis; verbal ability.
- **General Awareness:** Indian History, Geography, Polity, Economy, Science & Technology, Current Affairs.

---

### Study Resources & Practice Initiatives

- High-yield downloadable PDF study notes for every subject.
- Daily topic-wise practice quizzes and weekly full-length mock tests simulating the actual SSC-CGL exam pattern.
- Solved previous years' question papers with detailed step-by-step solutions.
- Doubts clearance sessions and one-to-one mentorship.
